# Tesla vs Webflow — Design Comparison Notes

| Dimension | Previous Tesla direction | Webflow direction |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Product-interface-first composition with a tighter, more controlled shell. | Wider editorial hero and pricing comparison surface; pricing becomes the dominant page rhythm. |
| Typography | More restrained product UI scale and neutral hierarchy. | Larger display headline, tighter tracking, and stronger type-led hierarchy while keeping weight at 600 or below. |
| Density | More evenly distributed product UI information. | Higher pricing-page density, using Free as the baseline and `Everything in …, plus:` to expose incremental value. |
| Card treatment | Stronger reliance on surface contrast and a single blue product accent; flatter hierarchy. | Predominantly white cards with hairline borders; Pro is emphasized by black border, `Recommended`, primary CTA, and only subtle elevation. |
| CTA hierarchy | Electric-blue primary action in the prior Tesla visual system. | Near-black primary CTA; secondary actions stay white with neutral hairline borders. |
| Color | Pure White / Light Ash with Carbon / Graphite and one blue accent. | White / near-black neutral system; color is intentionally absent from conversion controls. |
| Shape | Tight 4 px control geometry and flat product surfaces. | 4 px controls plus 8 px pricing/modal cards, retaining an engineered low-radius feel. |
| Modal | Flatter confirmation treatment with motion constrained mainly to color/opacity. | White 8 px dialog with restrained layered elevation and stronger page/dialog separation. |
| Motion | No scale/translate emphasis in the prior approved Tesla rules. | Functional low-amplitude backdrop fade plus very small modal translate/scale; exit is faster and reduced-motion is supported. |
| Overall personality | Automotive/product-system minimalism: controlled, quiet, interface-centric. | Professional SaaS pricing/editorial character: typographic, comparative, dense, and conversion-oriented without being promotional. |
